Got a tip for The Discovery Wire?

If you've spotted a published finding, an open question, or an instrument story we haven't covered — send it our way. The best tips have:

  • What was found or measured, and by whom
  • A link to the paper, institution release, or reputable outlet that reported it
  • What is still unknown or unsolved about it

How to send it

Message our Facebook page: The Discovery Wire. That is the only tip channel we monitor — please don't send personal information about yourself or anyone else.

What happens next

Every tip is checked against a published source before anything is written up. We report already-public science only: no unpublished claims, no predictions dressed as data, and open questions stay open. We don't pay for tips. If your tip checks out and becomes a story, it runs with the source credited at the bottom, like everything else on this wire.
