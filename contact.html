Contact The Discovery Wire

The Discovery Wire is a science desk covering already-public science: the experiments, the instruments, and the open questions. This page is how to reach us.

How to contact us

Message our Facebook page: The Discovery Wire. That is the contact channel we monitor — for questions about a story, corrections, story tips, and business or advertising inquiries, send us a message there and the desk will see it.

  • Story tips: see our Submit a Tip page for what makes a good tip and how we check it.
  • Corrections: if something we published looks wrong, message the page with the story link and what needs another look. Every article carries its source at the bottom, and we check corrections against that source.
  • Business and advertising inquiries: message the Facebook page above and say what you are proposing; the desk reads every message.

Where else to find us

  • This site: The Discovery Wire — the full story archive, newest first.
  • Facebook: The Discovery Wire — new stories land here, with discussion in the comments.

Please don't send personal information about yourself or anyone else. We report already-public science only, and we don't pay for tips.
